Feed yourself.
Every meal, on your terms.

Munchkin is a robotic feeding assistant for people with limited arm and hand movement. It brings each bite to you on its own, so you can eat at your own pace, at the same table, at the same time as everyone else.

Why it matters

Three times a day, every day.

Being able to feed yourself is one of the clearest lines between needing help and living independently. It happens at every meal, over a thousand times a year, and for millions of people it still depends on someone else.

The feeding devices on the market today still need someone beside them to scoop, aim, or press a button for each bite. That means a parent, partner, or caregiver spends the meal helping the machine instead of sharing it with you.

We started Munchkin so that mealtime can be yours again.

What makes Munchkin different

Built for independence, not just assistance.

Truly on your own

Munchkin finds the food on your plate and finds you. Nobody has to load the spoon or line up each bite, so the meal moves at your pace.

Eat together

When no one has to run the device, everyone eats at the same time. Your family sits down with you, and dinner gets to be dinner again.

Within reach

Assistive technology only helps if people can get it. We are designing Munchkin to cost far less than the feeding devices available today.

Who it's for

For anyone who can eat, but can't easily lift a spoon.

People with limited arm or hand movement

Anyone who can chew and swallow comfortably but finds it hard or impossible to bring food to their mouth.

Families and caregivers

More time at the table together, and one less task in a day that is already full.

Clinicians and therapists

A tool that supports independence goals at home, in care settings, and in rehab.

Including people living with

  • ALS
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Spinal cord injury
  • Spinal muscular atrophy
  • Muscular dystrophy
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Essential tremor
  • Traumatic brain injury
  • Arthrogryposis
  • Rett syndrome
  • Upper-limb amputation
  • and more
